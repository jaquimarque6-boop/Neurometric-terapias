---
name: Clinical access after reassignment
description: Neurometric Terapias' access rule for shared clinical records when patient assignments change.
---

Neurometric Terapias is an individual-use platform, not a shared clinical chart. A professional may access only patients currently assigned to their account and clinical records attributable to that same account. Do not grant access to another professional's records, even when both work with the patient. When assignment ends, the former professional loses access through the platform, including to their own older records for that patient. Preserve all historical data and original authorship without modification.

**Why:** The user corrected the earlier shared-history plan: the product must remain individual-use, with no team permissions, while retaining history and authorship after reassignment.

For historical rows without reliable author data, do not infer or rewrite authorship and do not delete the rows. Preserve current legitimate access through a separate authorization grant, not by pretending the current account authored the data. Do not disrupt the currently authorized professional's existing access. A patient's current assignment alone is not safe evidence for bulk-granting old records if past reassignments are unknown.

**How to apply:** Require both current patient assignment and record ownership wherever reliable author metadata exists. Apply this to session records, reports, files, attachments, downloads, appointments, and exports. For unattributed anamnesis, goals, and legacy records, design and validate a separate record-level access grant that preserves the currently authorized professional's existing use but is not automatically transferred on reassignment. Review how to establish grants for already reassigned patients; do not infer authorship, bulk-grant from current assignment when past assignments are unknown, or repurpose professional-directory links as account permissions.
